Nine pictures to carry into the interview

Every lesson so far, drawn as the picture you would put on the whiteboard. Practise until you can draw each one from memory in under three minutes while saying the notes out loud — that is most of a system design interview.

9 sketches Each one opens in Excalidraw ⤢ Full size on every sketch

How to practise with these

  1. Look at a sketch for one minute. Close it.
  2. Open a blank excalidraw.com and redraw it from memory, talking as you draw: what each box is, what happens when it dies.
  3. Compare with the original. Whatever you missed is what to re-read in that lesson’s notes.
  4. Then change the question (“now add payments”, “now 10× users”) and change the drawing to match.
